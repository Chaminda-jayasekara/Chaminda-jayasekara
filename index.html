The problem

Most small business websites
quietly lose you customers

You might not notice — but your visitors do, and they leave. Here's what's typically going wrong.

🐢
Slow to load
53% of visitors abandon a site after 3 seconds. Every second of delay costs real enquiries.
📱
Broken on mobile
Most of your traffic is on phones. A poor mobile experience loses them before they've read a word.
😕
Confusing layout
Visitors don't know what to do next. Confusion means they click away — straight to a competitor.
📣
No clear next step
If your site doesn't tell people to "book now" or "get a quote", they won't. Simple as that.
🎨
Dated design
First impressions happen in milliseconds. An old-looking site signals an untrustworthy business.
